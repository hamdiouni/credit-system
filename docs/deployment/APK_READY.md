## How to Install on Your Phone

### Method 1: USB Cable (Easiest)

1. **Connect your phone** to computer with USB cable
2. **Copy the APK file** to your phone:
   - Open File Explorer
   - Navigate to: `build\app\outputs\flutter-apk\`
   - Copy `app-release.apk` to your phone's Download folder
3. **On your phone**:
   - Open "Files" or "My Files" app
   - Go to Downloads folder
   - Tap on `app-release.apk`
   - Tap "Install"
   - If you see "Install blocked", tap "Settings" and enable "Allow from this source"
   - Tap "Install" again

### Method 2: Email

1. **Email the APK to yourself**:
   - Attach `app-release.apk` to an email
   - Send to your email address
2. **On your phone**:
   - Open the email
   - Download the attachment
   - Tap on the downloaded file
   - Tap "Install"

### Method 3: Cloud Storage (Google Drive, Dropbox, etc.)

1. **Upload APK** to Google Drive/Dropbox
2. **On your phone**:
   - Open Google Drive/Dropbox app
   - Download `app-release.apk`
   - Tap on the downloaded file
   - Tap "Install"

### Method 4: Bluetooth

1. **Right-click** on `app-release.apk`
2. Select **"Send to" → "Bluetooth device"**
3. Select your phone
4. Accept the file on your phone
5. Tap on the received file
6. Tap "Install"

## "Install from Unknown Sources" Warning

When you try to install, you'll see a security warning. This is **normal** for apps not from Google Play Store!

**To allow installation**:
1. When you see "Install blocked" or "For your security..."
2. Tap **"Settings"**
3. Enable **"Allow from this source"** or **"Install unknown apps"**
4. Go back and tap **"Install"** again

**This is safe** because:
- It's YOUR app that YOU built
- The code is on your computer
- No malware or viruses
- You can uninstall anytime

## Troubleshooting

### "App not installed"
- Make sure you have enough storage (need ~100 MB free)
- Try uninstalling any old version first
- Restart your phone and try again

### "Parse error"
- The APK file might be corrupted during transfer
- Copy the APK again from computer to phone
- Make sure you copied the complete file (63.7 MB)

### App crashes on startup
- Your phone needs Android 5.0 (Lollipop) or higher
- Try clearing app data: Settings → Apps → Credit App → Clear Data
