# 📱 Credit System - Complete User Guide (English)

## For Complete Beginners - Step by Step

This guide is written for anyone, even if you've never used a smartphone app before.

---

## 📥 Part 1: Installing the App

### On Mobile Phone (Android)

**Step 1: Get the App File**
1. You will receive a file called `app-release.apk`
2. This file can be sent to you via:
   - WhatsApp
   - Email
   - Bluetooth
   - USB cable from computer

**Step 2: Allow Installation**
1. Open your phone **Settings**
2. Find **Security** or **Privacy**
3. Look for **"Install unknown apps"** or **"Unknown sources"**
4. Turn it **ON** for the app you're using (like File Manager or Chrome)

**Step 3: Install the App**
1. Find the `app-release.apk` file on your phone
2. Tap on it
3. Tap **"Install"**
4. Wait for installation to complete
5. Tap **"Open"**

### On Computer (Windows)

**Step 1: Get the App File**
1. You will receive a folder with the app
2. Inside, find the file `credit_app.exe`

**Step 2: Run the App**
1. Double-click `credit_app.exe`
2. If Windows shows a warning:
   - Click **"More info"**
   - Click **"Run anyway"**
3. The app will open

## 🚀 Part 2: First Time Setup

### Creating Your Admin Account

**Step 1: Open the App**
- The app will show a welcome screen

**Step 2: Create Admin User**
1. You will see a form with:
   - **Name**: Type your name (example: "Ahmed")
   - **PIN**: Type 4 numbers (example: "1234")
   - **Confirm PIN**: Type the same 4 numbers again

2. Tap the **"Create Admin User"** button

3. You will see a success message

**Step 3: Login**
1. The app will show a PIN entry screen
2. Type your 4-digit PIN
3. Tap **"Login"**
4. You're in!

---

## 👥 Part 3: Adding Customers

### How to Add a New Customer

**Step 1: Open Customer List**
- You will see the main screen with a list of customers
- If this is your first time, the list will be empty

**Step 2: Add Customer**
1. Look at the bottom right corner
2. You will see a **blue circle button with a "+" sign**
3. Tap this button

**Step 3: Fill Customer Information**
1. **Name**: Type the customer's name
   - Example: "Mohamed Ali"
2. **Phone** (optional): Type phone number
   - Example: "98765432"
3. Tap **"Save"** button at the bottom

**Step 4: Success!**
- You will see a green message: "Customer added successfully"
- The customer now appears in your list

---

## 💰 Part 4: Adding Credit (When Customer Buys Items)

### When a Customer Buys Something on Credit

**Step 1: Find the Customer**
1. On the main screen, you will see your customer list
2. Tap on the customer's name

**Step 2: Add Credit**
1. You will see the customer's details
2. Tap the **"Add Credit"** button (green button)

**Step 3: Enter Purchase Details**
1. **Amount**: Type how much money they owe
   - Example: "50.500" (for 50 dinars and 500 millimes)

2. **Date**: The date is set to today
   - To change it, tap the calendar button

3. **Select Items** (optional):
   - Scroll down to see categories like:
     - 🌾 Grains & Staples
     - 🥛 Dairy Products
     - ☕ Beverages
     - 🥬 Vegetables
     - 🍎 Fruits
     - And more...
   - Tap on a category to expand it
   - Check the boxes for items they bought
   - Type the quantity for each item

4. **Note** (optional): Add any comment
   - Example: "Bought rice and oil"

5. Tap **"Save"** button

**Step 4: Done!**
- You will see: "Credit added successfully"
- The customer's debt is now updated

---

## 💵 Part 5: Adding Payment (When Customer Pays Money)

### When a Customer Pays You

**Step 1: Find the Customer**
1. Tap on the customer's name from the list

**Step 2: Add Payment**
1. Tap the **"Add Payment"** button (blue button)

**Step 3: Enter Payment Amount**
1. **Amount**: Type how much they paid
   - Example: "30.000"

2. **Date**: Set the payment date (default is today)

3. **Note** (optional): Add a comment
   - Example: "Partial payment"

**Step 4: Overpayment (If They Pay More Than They Owe)**

If the customer pays MORE than their debt:
1. You will see an error message
2. Check the box: **"Allow Overpayment"**
3. Tap **"Save"** again
4. A confirmation will appear showing:
   - How much they paid
   - How much they owed
   - How much will be saved as balance credit
5. Tap **"Confirm"**

**Step 5: Done!**
- You will see: "Payment added successfully"
- The customer's debt is reduced
- If they overpaid, they now have balance credit

## 📊 Part 6: Viewing Customer Information

### Understanding the Customer Screen

When you tap on a customer, you will see:

**1. Customer Information Card** (at the top)
- Customer name
- Phone number
- Date when customer was added

**2. Balance Section**
- **Current Debt**: How much they owe you (in red if they owe money)
- **Balance Credit**: Extra money they paid (in blue)

**3. Action Buttons**
- **Add Credit**: When they buy something
- **Add Payment**: When they pay you
- **Export PDF**: Create a report
- **Export CSV**: Create a spreadsheet

**4. Transaction History** (at the bottom)
- List of all credits and payments
- Each transaction shows:
  - Date and time
  - Type (Credit or Payment)
  - Amount
  - Items purchased (if any)
  - Notes

## 🔍 Part 7: Searching and Filtering

### Finding a Customer Quickly

**Search by Name:**
1. At the top of the customer list
2. You will see a search box
3. Type the customer's name
4. The list will show only matching customers

**Sort Customers:**
1. Tap the **sort icon** (three lines with dots)
2. Choose how to sort:
   - **Name**: Alphabetical order
   - **Debt: High to Low**: Customers who owe most first
   - **Debt: Low to High**: Customers who owe least first
   - **Newest First**: Recently added customers first
   - **Oldest First**: Old customers first

**Filter by Debt:**
1. Tap the **"With Debt"** chip/button
2. Only customers who owe money will show

### Creating a PDF Report

**Step 1: Open Customer Details**

**Step 2: Export PDF**
1. Tap the **"Export PDF"** button

- Total payments (how much they paid)

**Step 1: From Customer List**
1. Tap the **menu icon** (three dots) at the top

**Step 2: Save the File**
- The file will be saved/shared

## 💾 Part 10: Backup and Restore

### Creating a Backup (Saving Your Data)

**Why Backup?**
- To save all your data
- To transfer data to another phone/computer
- To keep a safe copy

**How to Backup:**
1. Open **Settings** (⚙️ icon)
2. Tap **"Backup & Restore"**
3. Tap **"Create Backup Now"**
4. Wait a few seconds
5. A file will be created with all your data

**Where is the Backup Saved?**
- **Mobile**: You choose where (WhatsApp, Email, Drive, etc.)
- **Computer**: In your Documents folder
- **Web**: Downloads folder

**Backup File Name:**
- Example: `credit_app_backup_2024-01-15.json`
- The date shows when you created it

### Restoring from Backup

**Step 1: Open Settings**
1. Tap **Settings** (⚙️)
2. Tap **"Backup & Restore"**

**Step 2: Choose Backup File**
1. Tap **"Restore from Backup"**
2. Find your backup file
3. Tap on it

**Step 3: Confirm**
1. You will see what's in the backup:
   - Number of customers
   - Number of transactions
2. Tap **"Restore"**
3. Wait for restoration to complete

**Step 4: Done!**
- All your data is restored
- You can see all customers and transactions

## ❓ Part 11: Common Questions

### Q: What if I forget my PIN?
**A:** 
- On computer: Run the file `reset_pin_simple.bat`
- On mobile: You may need to reinstall the app (your data will be lost unless you have a backup)

### Q: Can I use this on multiple devices?
**A:** Yes!
1. Create a backup on device 1
2. Send the backup file to device 2
3. Restore the backup on device 2

### Q: What happens if a customer overpays?
**A:** 
- The extra money is saved as "Balance Credit"
- Next time they buy something, the balance credit will automatically reduce their debt

### Q: Can I delete a customer?
**A:** 
- Yes, but only if they have no debt (debt = 0)
- If they owe money, you must collect payment first

### Q: Is my data safe?
**A:** 
- Yes! All data is stored on your device only
- No internet connection needed
- Create regular backups to be extra safe

### Q: Can I edit or delete a transaction?
**A:** 
- Yes! Tap on any transaction
- Choose "Edit" or "Delete"
- Only Admin users can delete transactions

## 🎯 Part 12: Quick Tips

### For Daily Use:

1. **Start of Day**: Open the app and check who owes money

2. **When Customer Buys**: 
   - Tap customer → Add Credit → Enter amount → Save

3. **When Customer Pays**: 
   - Tap customer → Add Payment → Enter amount → Save

4. **End of Day**: 
   - Check the Overview to see total debt
   - Create a backup if you made many changes

### Best Practices:

✅ **DO:**
- Create backups regularly (weekly or monthly)
- Add notes to transactions for clarity
- Check customer details before adding credit
- Use the search function to find customers quickly

❌ **DON'T:**
- Don't delete the app without creating a backup first
- Don't share your PIN with others
- Don't delete customers who still owe money

## 📞 Part 13: Getting Help

### If Something Goes Wrong:

1. **App won't open**: 
   - Restart your phone/computer
   - Reinstall the app

2. **Can't find a customer**: 
   - Use the search box
   - Check if you're filtering by "With Debt"

3. **Wrong amount entered**: 
   - Open the transaction
   - Tap "Edit"
   - Fix the amount
   - Save

4. **Lost all data**: 
   - If you have a backup, restore it
   - If no backup, data cannot be recovered
